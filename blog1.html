Where Our Ingredients Come From

May 17, 2026 · 3 min read

At The 30 Minute Dine fast food starts with fresh, locally sourced ingredients from Ontario farmers. We choose suppliers that prioritize quality over quantity with least invasive farming practices. Our meats are ethically sourced, raised free roaming and happy. Our vegetables are grown seasonally and nothing stays in the freezer longer than it has to. We believe that a great meal is more than the end result but a combination of everything that goes into it. When you choose The 30 Minute Dine you aren't compromising on quality or speed.
